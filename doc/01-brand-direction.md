# 01 — Brand Direction

**Acqua Fun Bugesera**  
Creative website visual identity  
Status: Approved direction · Hero implementation in progress

---

## 1. Brand truth

Acqua Fun Bugesera is a **sunlit family water playground in Bugesera, Rwanda** — not a mega coastal inflatable park, not a spa resort, and not a generic swimming-pool club.

The park’s real promise, drawn from its facilities and Instagram material:

> A colourful day out where families splash, slide, float and play together — wave pool energy, bubble foam joy, kids’ toys, and big blue water under Rwandan sky.

The website must sell that **feeling** first, then make the visit easy to plan.

---

## 2. What we take from the references (without copying)

### Primary reference — AquaFun Dubai (`aquafun.ae`)

**Steal the principles, not the look.**

| Principle | How AquaFun Dubai uses it | How we adapt for Bugesera |
|---|---|---|
| Feeling before logistics | Hero sells joy/scale before ticket math | Hero sells splash, family energy, Bugesera place |
| Oversized editorial headlines | Huge playful display type | Same energy; different letterforms and voice |
| Integrated 3D objects | Inflatable-inspired props as composition | Life vests, rings, balls, starfish — anchored to layouts |
| Bold colour + soft atmosphere | Saturated accents on soft water fields | Our token palette; calmer white/deep-ocean rests |
| Clear primary CTA | Ticket / visit always obvious | “Plan your visit” + contact always visible |

**Do not copy:** Dubai Marina skyline framing, Guinness/scale messaging, floating-obstacle park structure, their exact colour system, or their section rhythm.

### Secondary reference — Suntago (`suntago.com`)

**Steal organisation and visitor clarity.**

| Principle | How Suntago uses it | How we adapt |
|---|---|---|
| Zones for different needs | Jungle FUN / SPA / Saunaria | Facility zones: Wave, Slides, Bubbles, Family, Super, Toys |
| Visit planning first | Calendars, maps, group paths | Practical visit strip: hours, location, contact, “what to bring” |
| Audience routing | Kids / groups / relax | Families, kids, thrill seekers — without inventing zones we don’t have |
| Calm functional pages | Info dense but scannable | Visit, contact, FAQ use quieter layouts |

**Do not copy:** Tropical spa dual-world concept, sauna/zone calendars, or festival-event density.

## 3. Positioning

### Brand line (working)

**Acqua Fun Bugesera — Splash days for the whole family.**

### Personality

| Trait | Expression | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Playful | Rounded type, bounce motion, colourful props | Never childish or chaotic |
| Sunny | Yellow accents, bright photography, warm light | Never neon / festival overload |
| Welcoming | Clear CTAs, family imagery, simple visit info | Never corporate leisure-generic |
| Aquatic | Blues, water whites, soft ripples | Never stock “underwater blue void” |
| Local | Bugesera / Kanzenze Road, real park photos | Never generic tropical stock paradise |

### Tone of voice

- Short, energetic, warm.
- Speaks to parents and kids without baby-talk.
- Prefers verbs: splash, slide, float, play, gather.
- Avoids hype clichés: “ultimate experience,” “world-class destination,” “unforgettable journey.”

**Example headlines (direction, not final copy):**

- Hero: **Jump into the splash.**
- Facilities: **Six ways to get wet.**
- Visit: **Come ready to play.**
- CTA: **Plan your day in Bugesera.**

## 4. Visual identity concept

### Name of the system

**“Splash Composition”**

A visual system where **photography is the stage** and **inflatable-inspired objects + organic water shapes are the supporting cast**. Layouts feel composed like posters and playground scenes — not like SaaS marketing pages with floating ornaments.

### Core idea

Every major viewport should feel like a **cropped moment from the park**: water, people, colour, and one or two purposeful decorative objects that belong in that scene (a life vest near Wave Pool, a ring near Super Pool, a starfish near kids’ play).

### Differentiation from generic water-park sites

| Generic (reject) | Our direction |
|---|---|
| Card grids of attractions with icons | Asymmetric facility compositions with photo crops |
| Rainbow gradient backgrounds everywhere | Section-specific colour roles; mostly water white / deep ocean / single accent |
| Scattered PNG stickers in corners | Anchored 3D objects tied to content meaning |
| Inter / Roboto / system UI feel | Fredoka display + Plus Jakarta Sans UI |
| Purple leisure gradients / cream editorial tropes | Ocean–sun–coral playground system |
| Hero with 6 competing messages | Brand + one headline + one line + one CTA group + one dominant image |

## 5. Logo & wordmark direction

### Existing mark (from park materials)

The Instagram poster shows a bubbly 3D “Acqua Fun” wordmark with splash energy (blue/orange). That spirit is correct for the brand.

### Website recommendation

1. **Primary digital mark:** Clean vector adaptation of the existing wordmark (or a refined 2D sibling) for crisp UI use.
2. **Lockup:** `Acqua Fun` primary + `Bugesera` as a smaller supporting line in Plus Jakarta Sans — never competing with the playful mark.
3. **Do not** rebuild a completely different logo for the website launch without brand-owner approval.
4. **Usage:** Hero and footer carry the brand at large scale; nav uses a compact lockup.

### Brand test (mandatory)

> If you remove the navigation, the first viewport must still unmistakably belong to Acqua Fun Bugesera — through brand scale, palette, photography, and voice.

---

## 6. Colour philosophy

Palette tokens are defined in `02-design-system.md`. Directionally:

- **Ocean blue + splash cyan** = water, trust, primary actions.
- **Sunshine yellow** = joy, highlights, kids energy (sparingly).
- **Coral + bubble pink** = accent moments, slides, playfulness (never both dominating the same section).
- **Deep ocean** = strong type, night/footer, contrast anchors.
- **Water white** = breathing room and readability.

**Rule:** Each section uses a **primary field + one accent**. Not the whole rainbow.

---

## 7. Typography philosophy

- **Display:** Fredoka (preferred over Baloo 2) — rounded, bold, playground-confident without becoming cartoon-letter soup.
- **Body / UI:** Plus Jakarta Sans — modern, readable, friendly; better geometric clarity for forms and nav than Manrope for this brand.

Hierarchy must stay editorial: one giant line, one support line, then quiet body. Never three competing display sizes in one viewport.

---

## 8. Imagery direction

### Authentic photography first

Use real park material wherever possible:

- Wave Pool (life-vest crowds, moving water)
- Bubbles Pool (foam play)
- Family Pool / play structure (slides, tipping bucket, sea creatures)
- Super Pool (open blue water)
- Toys & Games / children’s equipment (spring riders, play area)
- Landscape cues: palms, hills, bright Bugesera sky

### Cropping language

- Bold crops into water, faces laughing, slide mouths, foam texture.
- Avoid centred postcard photos in rounded cards.
- Prefer full-bleed or edge-bleeding photography with organic masks (soft wave edges, not hard circles everywhere).

### What not to use

- Generic stock pools from other countries.
- Over-filtered “travel influencer” grading.
- Invented attractions or CGI rides that do not exist at the park.

## 9. Decorative object strategy (brand level)

Objects are **set dressing**, not confetti.

Approved motif family (derived from park reality + aquatic playground language):

1. Inflatable ring  
2. Beach ball  
3. Life vest  
4. Starfish  
5. Soft water splash / droplet shapes  
6. Optional: seahorse / dolphin silhouette language *only* when echoing real play-structure décor

**Composition rules:**

- Max **1–2 objects** per major section.
- Objects must **intersect** type or photo edges intentionally (overlap, peek, cast soft shadow).
- Objects never block CTAs, phone numbers, or key headlines.
- Prefer depth: large soft object in foreground + smaller echo in mid-ground.

Full asset list lives in `05-content-and-assets.md`.

---

## 10. Motion philosophy (brand level)

Motion should feel like **water + play**, not like a motion-design showreel.

- Soft float / bob for objects  
- Gentle parallax on hero layers  
- Splash-like page section reveals  
- Confident but calm hover states on CTAs  

Details in `04-animation-guidelines.md`.

---

## 11. Audience framing

Design for:

1. **Families in Kigali & Bugesera** planning a day out  
2. **Parents** scanning for facilities, safety, contact, location  
3. **Groups / schools / parties** needing a clear contact path  
4. **Returning visitors** looking for what’s open / how to reach the park  

Do not design primarily for design-award judges. Creativity serves destination desire + visit conversion.

---

## 12. Critical anti-patterns for this project

Reject these even if they feel “fun”:

1. Dashboard-like first viewport (stats, hours, map, promos all at once)  
2. Icon-row “feature strips” as the main facilities presentation  
3. Endless identical rounded cards  
4. Sticky promo banners with historical prices from old posters  
5. Emoji decoration in UI  
6. Dark-mode-first leisure aesthetic  
7. Purple/indigo AI-default gradients  
8. Overbuilt multi-CTA hero (“Book / Watch / Explore / Learn more”)  
9. Inventing attractions beyond: Wave Pool, Water Slides, Bubbles Pool, Family Pool, Super Pool, Toys & Games, Children’s play equipment, Family entertainment  
10. Treating Instagram poster prices (e.g. 12K / 8K promo) as current website pricing without confirmation  

---

## 13. Success criteria for the brand direction

The direction is successful if reviewers can say:

1. It feels like **entering a colourful aquatic playground**.  
2. It feels **specific to Bugesera**, not a clone of AquaFun Dubai.  
3. Visit actions (location, contact, plan visit) are **obvious within seconds**.  
4. Facilities shown are **real and recognisable**.  
5. Decoration feel **composed**, not sprinkled.  
6. Mobile still feels premium and playful, not a collapsed card stack.
