## 1. Content principles

1. **Real over invented** — only claim facilities proven by park materials.  
2. **Desire then details** — emotional copy first; logistics second.  
3. **No stale promo facts** — historical poster prices/dates are not website truth until reconfirmed.  
4. **Readable over clever** — wordplay is welcome; confusion is not.  
5. **Local specificity** — Bugesera, Kanzenze Road, Rwandan family day-out context.

## 4. Messaging architecture

### Brand promise (working)

Acqua Fun Bugesera is Bugesera’s colourful family water playground — waves, slides, bubbles, pools and play under the sun.

### Value pillars

1. **Splash variety** — multiple real water zones, not one pool.  
2. **Family-first** — kids’ play + shared water time.

### 6.3 Photography style guide

- Natural daylight; preserve cyan water and saturated play colours  
- Include real guests when permissions allow  
- Prefer candid motion over posed brochure stiffness  
- Keep horizon/environment (palms, hills) when it says “Bugesera”  
- Avoid heavy vignettes and fake HDR  

### 6.4 Cropping guidance for web

- Hero: full-bleed cinematic crop, people as energy not tiny distant dots  
- Facilities: alternate wide establishing shots with tight joy crops  
- Avoid default centre-square thumbnails for everything  

### 6.5 Rights & consent

- Confirm usage rights for all guest-facing photos  
- Prefer model releases for identifiable children on marketing pages  
- Poster screenshot (`info.png`) is reference — redesign info; don’t ship the flyer as a web section

### 7.2 Optional secondary

- Seahorse / dolphin motifs **only** as subtle echoes of on-site play décor  
- Mascot La Bubu **only** if brand wants continuity with social campaigns  

### 7.3 Production specs (for later)

- Transparent WebP/PNG at 1x/2x  
- Clean edges, soft contact shadows separate from asset when possible  
- Neutral lighting consistent across set  
- Avoid cheap clipart plastic look — aim for soft toy / inflatable product realism  
- Keep file sizes lean for mobile  

### 7.4 Composition placement map (starter)

| Section | Object | Placement idea |
|---|---|---|---|

| Visit / Contact | None or one muted object far from inputs | Protect usability |

## 12. Legal / trust notes

- Do not imply hotel/resort services unless confirmed as part of Acqua Fun operations.  
- Do not reuse other parks’ claims (largest, Guinness, etc.).  
- Mark user-generated quotes only if real and approved.  
- If collecting form data, prepare a basic privacy note.

## 13. Content success criteria

- Every facility on the site exists in real life  
- No outdated promo price appears as current fact  
- Contact and location are accurate and prominent  
- Photography looks like Bugesera’s park, not stock  
- Decorative assets feel native to compositions  
- Copy sounds like a playful destination brand, not a corporate brochure
